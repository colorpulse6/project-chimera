The feet reached every mark. The place still missed its painting.

Run 19b completed the check that [run 18 could not begin](/updates/2026-11-12-the-stair-reaches-the-terrace/). In the internal package, Kai travelled to High Pass, walked from arrival to the shore and back, and reached all seventy-eight targets in order. The worst recorded errors were fifty-one centimetres across the ground and sixteen centimetres vertically. No sea, fall or catch event was recorded. Both packaged runs exited cleanly, and Nic’s original save and settings stayed unchanged.

The editor checks passed too. All four distant-view cameras passed their strict rules on the first attempt; camera 05 needed no exception this time. The fixed scene comparisons passed.

**Nic played the package and did not accept the approach. The candidate has been restored out of the live project.** The build remains as evidence. Demo 0.1.0 is unchanged.

## What is missing

At the lakeshore, painting 03 calls for houses stacked on rocks above the dock. The candidate did not supply that cottage cluster. Above it, painting 04 calls for a narrow winding path with the dead pine. The candidate’s wide straight stair and pale box rocks did not match it.

Nic also repeated the larger direction: open world, not long raised roads over water. The ground band is still unfinished. A completed test walk does not make that journey complete.

These are art and place findings, not a withdrawal of the recorded traversal result. Ordinary companion travel and full-body turning checks also remained open; the scripted walk was never their substitute.

## The next place must look like its painting

Nic’s standing standard now requires a painting beside an actual game frame from the same viewpoint before work is called done. Stops need surfaces and close-up dressing that hold up at walking distance: props, plants, wear and clutter. Pale box rocks, blank surfaces and untextured placeholders do not qualify as finished.

The first source replacement was sent back before an engine window. No replacement frame is published here. A later source proposal still needs review, runtime checks and Nic’s art decision.

The western mainland source boards received Nic’s approval separately. Their in-game comparison and final acceptance remain ahead; that choice does not approve the rejected lakeshore and stair or complete the ground connection.

The [roadmap](/roadmap/) keeps those separate pieces open. The next approach needs to be a place worth reaching, as well as a route that can be walked.
